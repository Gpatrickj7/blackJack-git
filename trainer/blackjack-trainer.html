Count drill

Cards flip one at a time. Keep the Hi-Lo count in your head: 2 to 6 are +1, 7 to 9 are 0, tens and aces are -1. Enter the count when they stop.

True count

The true count is the running count per deck still in the shoe. Read the decks left off the discard tray, divide, and round the way your settings say.
